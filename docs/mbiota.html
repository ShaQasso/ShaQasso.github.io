Mbiota: Remission

Keep a gut in remission for six months

I turned the biology I work on into a small browser game. You look after a colony of Bacteroides through six months. Phages attack in volleys, the gut wall flares, and between waves you garden the colony with food and drugs.

1 · The colony

A colony of cells in three capsule types

Each colour is a capsule type, the coat a bacterium shows to its surroundings, and each has one job. Blue stands for a capsule like PSA of Bacteroides fragilis, which calms the immune system: it cools the wall it faces. Yellow resists immune cells. Green slows flares: the more green, the slower they build. A big patch of one colour grows a protective coat, but a bite into it takes far more.

2 · The phages

Phages infect one capsule type

Phages arrive in volleys and wait at the rim before they launch. Each infects only the colour of its head and spreads through neighbours of that colour. Your one control in a wave is to turn the colony, so they land on a different colour.

3 · The wall

Blue calms a flare, at a cost

When a section of the wall flares, turn blue cells toward it and they pulse out to cool it. The flare also sends immune cells that scrape whatever faces them, so calming the wall costs you blue. Let inflammation stay too high for too long and the run ends.

4 · The garden

Between waves you garden

Each month you drag one card to where it should work. Fibre, mucin or starch grows one colour in a spot you choose. An antibiotic clears a patch that got too big. Mesalamine cools one section of the wall, and a steroid cools all of it but raises the baseline.

What is real and what is simplified

The idea is real: Bacteroides switch capsule types by flipping DNA, and phages, inflammation and diet push those switches. PSA is a real anti-inflammatory capsule. The game uses three clean colours, fixed speeds and a lot of simplification. It is a way into the idea, not a model of a gut.

Where it started

Mbiota Adventures, the first version

The idea began as Mbiota Adventures, a browser game about diversity, fitness and host defence. I built it iteratively with Gemini 3 and tuned the event logic and diversity dynamics by hand. This version grew out of it. The original is kept as it was.
